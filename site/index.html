What you get

Four things, off one folder of demos.

Upload a season's matches once. Everything below comes out of the same parse, so there is nothing else to set up and nothing to keep in sync.

Per match

Match reports

One page per match: how the rounds actually went, where the score moved, and the handful of moments worth pulling up at practice.

Per team

Recurring patterns

The things your team does over and over without deciding to: same site off the same utility, same rotation trigger, same buy after the same loss. Counted across every match, not eyeballed from one.

Per player

Player stats

Role as observed rather than as labelled, weapon breakdown, opening duels taken and won, and where each player actually stands at freeze-end.

Per team

Team stats

Map pool with round records rather than just match records, side splits, economy habits, and how often you convert the situations you get into.

And one link

Your roster reads it without making accounts.

One address opens your season read-only: the stats, the patterns, every match report. Send it to five players and a graduate assistant and none of them sign up, install anything, or ask you for a seat. Revoke it whenever you like.

Every other team tool in this game sells seats, with a minimum of five. A seat minimum is a purchase order and a conversation with whoever holds the budget. This is a link.

On purpose

There is no ranking of you against professional teams here, and there will not be. Every number on this site is your rounds measured against your rounds.

The whole promise

Every number has a denominator.

Most analytics tools score your team against a population you have never played. A collegiate roster measured against professionals comes back "below average at everything": true, useless, and slightly insulting. Counterplay does not do that.

No predictions

Counted, not modelled

Tendencies come from parsing demos and counting what happened. There is no estimate anywhere in the scouting section, so there is nothing to be wrong about.

No borrowed baselines

Only teams from the same server

When your team is compared to another, both were in the same match, in the same tier, against the same opposition. Any bias applies to both sides and cancels.

Small samples say so

"Thin" instead of a fake rate

Below eight chances you get raw counts and a warning label, never a percentage. A rate off four rounds is noise wearing a percent sign.

Auditable

Round numbers printed

Every claim lists the rounds it came from. Open the demo, watch round 14, and check us. If a line is wrong you can see that it is wrong.

How it works

Three things, none of which are your job.

  1. 01

    Tell us who you are

    A team name, an email and a password. That is the whole setup, and it takes about a minute.

  2. 02

    Drop in your demos

    Whatever you have: league matches, scrims, a whole season at once. Demos expire, so save the ones worth keeping now: FACEIT and Valve both delete match demos after a few weeks.

  3. 03

    You get a link, and it stays current

    Stats, patterns and a report per match, updating as you add matches. Send the link to your roster; no accounts for them to make, nothing for anyone to install.

What we do with them, in plain language.

If your athletics department or IT office asks, this section is the answer, and you are welcome to forward it. The full version is the privacy policy.

  • ✓We parse them and keep the round data. Positions, buys, outcomes: the numbers that build league baselines.
  • ✓Your team's report is yours. We do not show one team's report to another team.
  • ✓We do not publish or sell demo files. They are not redistributed, and they are not listed anywhere public.
  • ✓Baselines are aggregate. League-wide reference numbers are situation rates by map, side and tier, not profiles of individual players who never signed up.
  • ✓Your data stays yours. Ask for your team's data to be deleted and it is deleted, no conversation required.
