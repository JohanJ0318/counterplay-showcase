The whole promise

Every number has a denominator.

Most analytics tools score your team against a population you have never played. A collegiate roster measured against professionals comes back "below average at everything": true, useless, and slightly insulting. Counterplay does not do that.

No predictions

Counted, not modelled

Tendencies come from parsing demos and counting what happened. There is no estimate anywhere in the scouting section, so there is nothing to be wrong about.

No borrowed baselines

Only teams from the same server

When your team is compared to another, both were in the same match, in the same tier, against the same opposition. Any bias applies to both sides and cancels.

Small samples say so

"Thin" instead of a fake rate

Below eight chances you get raw counts and a warning label, never a percentage. A rate off four rounds is noise wearing a percent sign.

Auditable

Round numbers printed

Every claim lists the rounds it came from. Open the demo, watch round 14, and check us. If a line is wrong you can see that it is wrong.

Beta: read this before you trust it

Decision scoring, and what beta means.

Everything else on this page is counting. This one is a model, so it gets its own label and its own warning.

It answers a question no other Counter-Strike tool answers: not how did we play, but which decision cost us that round.

3v1 post-plant, Inferno, round 14, peeked pit at 0:19 · cost 21 points of win probability

The engine estimates how often a situation like that one is won, then compares what you did to what teams in the same tier actually did from the same spot. Rounds you were comfortably winning and threw away surface first, because those are the ones worth talking about.

Why it is beta: the comparison is only as good as the pool it compares against, and the league pool is still being built by the teams using this. Until it is big enough, decision scoring shows its sample size on every row and stays out of anything that looks like a grade. If it says something that does not match what you remember, tell us. That feedback is worth more to us than the subscription would be.

Your demos

What we do with them, in plain language.

If your athletics department or IT office asks, this section is the answer, and you are welcome to forward it.

  • ✓We parse them and keep the round data. Positions, buys, outcomes: the numbers that build league baselines.
  • ✓Your team's report is yours. We do not show one team's report to another team.
  • ✓We do not publish or sell demo files. They are not redistributed, and they are not listed anywhere public.
  • ✓Baselines are aggregate. League-wide reference numbers are situation rates by map, side and tier, not profiles of individual players who never signed up.
  • ✓Leave and it stops. Ask us to delete your team's data and we delete it, no conversation required.
Not yet

Things this does not do today, so nobody finds out the hard way: no live in-game overlay, no 3D replay, no practice servers, and no scouting of opponents whose demos you do not have. There is also no ranking of you against professional teams. That one is deliberate and it is not coming. If you need those, Skybox, Refrag and CS2.CAM each do one of them well.
