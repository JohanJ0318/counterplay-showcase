# Design references

Put anything here that shows what you want the site to look like. Then say
"look at design/refs" and I will read it.

## What to put in

**Screenshots.** PNG or JPG, any name. I read images directly. Screenshots of
a real product beat a description of one every time, and a screenshot with a
circle drawn round the bit you like beats a plain one.

**Whole templates.** Download a template and unzip the folder in here. I can
read the HTML and CSS and lift the parts that fit rather than guessing from a
picture.

**A links file.** A plain text file of URLs works too. I can open each one,
screenshot it, and read its real CSS: actual spacing, type scale and colour
values rather than my estimate of them from an image.

## What is most useful

Screenshots of **products people use every week**, not landing pages.

Counterplay's landing page is already good. What needs work is the signed-in
app, and an app has the opposite job from a marketing page: show forty numbers
to somebody who has seen them before, without tiring them out. Small type,
tight spacing, tables that stay readable.

So: the logged-in screens of Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Height, Leetify. Not
their homepages.

## What does not work

Figma links, and anything behind a login I do not have. Screenshot those
instead.
